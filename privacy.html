Privacy Policy

Ziptaa ("Ziptaa," "we," "us," or "our") provides an AI receptionist service that helps small businesses answer calls, book appointments, and manage customer communications. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have — including specific information about text message (SMS) communications.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with a business that uses Ziptaa — for example, by calling to book, reschedule, or cancel an appointment — we may collect:

  • Your name and phone number
  • Appointment details (date, time, service requested)
  • Call recordings and transcripts, used to provide and improve the service
  • Information you provide directly during the call, such as the reason for your visit

If you are a business owner using the Ziptaa dashboard, we also collect your account information, business details, and calendar data you connect to the service (such as Google Calendar).

2. SMS / Text Messaging

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Message frequency varies depending on your appointment activity — typically you will receive one confirmation message per booking, and one message if that appointment is later rescheduled or cancelled. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out of future text messages, or reply HELP for assistance.

Text messages are sent by the AI receptionist on behalf of the business you contacted, using the phone number you provided during your call, after you verbally agree to receive a text confirmation.

3. How We Use Information

  • To book, confirm, reschedule, or cancel appointments on behalf of the business you contacted
  • To send appointment-related text message confirmations and updates
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Ziptaa platform
  • To provide customer support to businesses using Ziptaa

4. How We Share Information

We share information with the specific small business you contacted, so they can manage your appointment. We use third-party service providers to operate our platform, including:

  • Twilio, for placing and receiving phone calls and text messages
  • Vapi, for AI voice call handling
  • Google Calendar, for appointment scheduling, when connected by the business
  • Supabase, for secure data storage
  • Stripe, for billing (business account holders only)

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Google User Data

If a business owner connects Google Calendar to Ziptaa, we ask for access through Google sign-in. On Google's permission screen this appears as three items: view and edit events on your calendars, see your calendars, and see your primary Google Account email address.

What we access and why: We read your list of calendars so you can choose which one Ziptaa uses. We read the busy and free times on that calendar so the AI receptionist can offer open appointment slots when a caller asks to book. We create, change and delete the calendar events for appointments that Ziptaa books, reschedules or cancels. We use your Google Account email only to show you which account is connected. We also store the ID of that calendar and the ID of each event we create, so we can update or remove it later.

How we use it: We use Google data only to provide these features to the business that connected it. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to develop, improve or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Who sees it: While a call is in progress, the open time slots, without event titles or attendee details, are passed to our voice AI provider so the assistant can offer times to the caller. Our database provider stores the connection details. Our team does not read your calendar data except with your permission to give support, to investigate abuse or security issues, or when the law requires it. We do not share Google data with anyone else.

Limited Use: Ziptaa's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting and deleting: You can disconnect Google Calendar at any time from Settings in the Ziptaa dashboard. When you do, we ask Google to revoke our access and we remove the access keys we store, so we can no longer read or change your calendar. Deleting your Ziptaa account does the same. You can also remove Ziptaa's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Appointments that Ziptaa already added stay in your own Google Calendar after you disconnect, and Ziptaa will not change them.

6. Data Retention

We retain call and appointment information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service and to comply with applicable business and legal obligations.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. Connections to Ziptaa are encrypted, the access keys we keep for connected services are stored in encrypted form, and each business can see only its own information. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices

  • Reply STOP to any text message from Ziptaa to opt out of future messages
  • Reply HELP to any text message for assistance
  • Contact the business you booked with directly to request your information be updated or removed
  • Contact us at support@ziptaa.com with any privacy questions or requests

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the date at the top of this page. If we make a material change, we will tell business account holders in the dashboard or by email. We will not use Google user data for a new purpose without asking you first.
